About Me

Electrical & Electronic Engineering graduate from RUET and Lecturer at East Delta University, Chattogram. My research centers on multi-pulse rectifier design, auxiliary injection techniques, and harmonic mitigation in AC-DC conversion systems — addressing power quality challenges in modern grids with increasing renewable energy integration. I am actively pursuing graduate study opportunities to extend this work toward grid-integrated power electronic systems.

* Peer-reviewed journal publications

New

[1] M. A. Malek and A. S. M. J. Hossain, "Passive auxiliary injection circuits for pulse tripling in three parallel-connected rectifiers," Journal of Electronic Science and Technology, 2026, Art. no. 100360.

Proposed novel PAICs enabling 9-, 18-, and 54-pulse rectifier configurations without phase-shifting transformers — achieving THD reduction down to 1.90% with threefold increase in output-voltage pulses. Validated via MATLAB/Simulink for high-current industrial applications.

doi: 10.1016/j.jnlest.2026.100360

[2] A. S. M. J. Hossain, M. A. Malek, and M. A. G. Khan, "A simple 24-pulse star rectifier using three new auxiliary injection circuits for harmonic mitigation," e-Prime – Advances in Electrical Engineering, Electronics and Energy, vol. 13, Art. no. 101086, 2025.

Three passive injection circuits enhance pulse count from 6 to 24, achieving 7.711% input current THD. Cost-efficient design validated through mathematical analysis, modeling, and simulation.

doi: 10.1016/j.prime.2025.101086

Designed phase detector and IGBT driver circuits using Arduino Mega, optocouplers, and PWM generation. Prototype built and tested in RUET Electronics Lab — verified via oscilloscope gate-pulse analysis.
