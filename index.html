Lecturer in Electrical & Electronic Engineering at East Delta University, Chattogram, and a published researcher in power electronics and power quality. My work focuses on multi-pulse rectifier topologies, passive auxiliary injection techniques, and THD optimization in AC-DC conversion systems with ongoing investigations into ML-assisted leakage inductance modelling and diode fault detection in high-pulse converter systems, in collaboration with my research supervisor. Alongside teaching Power Electronics and Electronic Devices & Circuits, I am actively building toward graduate research in power electronics, electric vehicle drive systems, and renewable energy integration areas where my simulation, hardware, and publication experience directly contribute.

* Peer-reviewed journal publications

New

[1] M. A. Malek and A. S. M. J. Hossain, "Passive auxiliary injection circuits for pulse tripling in three parallel-connected rectifiers," Journal of Electronic Science and Technology, 2026, Art. no. 100360.

Proposed novel PAICs enabling 9-, 18-, and 54-pulse rectifier configurations without phase-shifting transformers — achieving THD reduction down to 1.90% with threefold increase in output-voltage pulses. Validated via MATLAB/Simulink for high-current industrial applications.

doi: 10.1016/j.jnlest.2026.100360

[2] A. S. M. J. Hossain, M. A. Malek, and M. A. G. Khan, "A simple 24-pulse star rectifier using three new auxiliary injection circuits for harmonic mitigation," e-Prime – Advances in Electrical Engineering, Electronics and Energy, vol. 13, Art. no. 101086, 2025.

Three passive injection circuits enhance pulse count from 6 to 24, achieving 7.711% input current THD. Cost-efficient design validated through mathematical analysis, modeling, and simulation.

doi: 10.1016/j.prime.2025.101086

ML-Assisted Leakage Inductance Modelling

Applying machine learning techniques to model and predict leakage inductance behaviour in multi-pulse rectifier transformers — improving accuracy of harmonic analysis beyond classical analytical methods.
